Good to know

How do I get there?

Harbour Fields is a 15-minute walk from Harbour Road station, with free shuttles every 10 minutes. Bike parking is free and cars are strongly discouraged.

Can I camp?

Yes. The weekend + camp ticket includes a pitch in our quiet field. Bring a tent or rent one ready-pitched, with a sleeping mat and everything.

Is the festival accessible?

Yes. We have step-free routes, viewing platforms at every stage, accessible toilets and a quiet space. Contact us ahead and we’ll help plan your visit.

What about the environment?

Reusable cups only, vegetable-first food, solar-powered stages and a litter-pick pledge for every camper.

Can children come?

Under-12s go free with an adult, and there’s a kids’ garden on Saturday and Sunday afternoons.
