FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do my clients need an account to pay?

No. They get a secure link, review the invoice and pay by card or bank transfer without signing up for anything.

Can I import my existing clients and invoices?

Yes. Upload a CSV or connect your accounting tool and your history comes with you in a few minutes.

How do payments work?

Ledgerly connects to your payment provider and your bank. Funds go straight to you, and invoices are marked paid automatically.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose a plan or drop to the free tier. Nothing is charged without your say-so, and you can cancel any time.
