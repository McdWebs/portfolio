Welcome

Twelve rooms above a quiet bay. Salt on the windows, linen on the beds, and nothing at all on the schedule. Come to do less, and stay until it feels like enough.

The bay

One beach, no timetable.

Our beach is a five-minute walk down a path lined with rosemary. There are no sun-lounger queues, no kids’ club and no loudspeakers. Only tide, towels and long lunches.
