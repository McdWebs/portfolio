How we bake

Three days, three steps.

1

Feed

Our starter is nine years old and fed twice daily. It’s the whole flavor, so we treat it like a pet.

2

Fold

Slow hand-folds and a cold 36-hour proof build the open crumb and the deep, tangy flavor.

3

Fire

Into a steam-injected deck oven at dawn. Listen closely: a good loaf crackles as it cools.

We bake honest bread for a handful of streets, using local flour, real butter and a lot of patience.
